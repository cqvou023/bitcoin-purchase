## Instant buy versus spot trading

The easiest route for a first Bitcoin purchase is usually the Buy interface. You enter an amount, choose a payment method, review the quote, and confirm.

Spot trading gives you more control over the order price. You can choose a market order or place a limit order at a price you specify. That flexibility comes with a busier interface and a higher chance of misunderstanding the order type.

### Use instant buy when:

- You are making a small first purchase.
- You care more about simplicity than fine-tuning execution.
- You want to pay through an available payment provider.
- You have reviewed the quoted BTC amount and total cost.

### Consider spot trading when:

- You already understand order books.
- You want to compare maker and taker fees.
- You plan to trade more frequently.
- You want to set a specific limit price.
- You are funding the account in advance and do not need instant settlement.

The key comparison is not just “simple versus advanced.” Instant-buy routes may include a spread in the quoted price, while spot orders have explicit maker or taker fees and may fill at a different price during market movement. OKX advises users to check the rate and applicable fee in the order panel before submitting a trade.

## Common mistakes when buying BTC

### Buying before checking the final quote

Bitcoin prices move continuously. The number shown before confirmation may change, and the amount of BTC received can be affected by the quote, spread, and fee.

Always review the final order screen rather than estimating the amount from a public BTC price page.

### Confusing Bitcoin with a Bitcoin-related product

Make sure the asset is **BTC** and that you understand whether you are buying Bitcoin itself, converting another cryptocurrency into BTC, or using a derivative product.

A spot purchase of BTC is different from a leveraged position or perpetual contract. Leverage adds liquidation risk and is unnecessary for a basic buy-and-hold purchase.

### Sending BTC to the wrong network or address

Before withdrawing, verify the destination wallet and supported network. A blockchain transfer is generally irreversible once sent to an incorrect address.

For a first withdrawal, a small test transfer can reduce the risk of losing a larger amount because of an address or network mistake.

### Leaving account security until later

At minimum, use a unique password, enable two-factor authentication, and be careful with login links and verification codes. Never share a one-time password or withdrawal confirmation code with someone claiming to be support.
